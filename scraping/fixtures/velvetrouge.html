Velvet Rouge by Fragrance World is a Floral Woody Musk fragrance for women and men. Velvet Rouge was launched in 2023. Top notes are White Tea and Pink Pepper; middle notes are Rose, Iris and Jasmine; base notes are Patchouli, Vetiver and Musk.

Velvet Rouge by Fragrance World is a unisex fragrance that blends floral and woody musk elements. It opens with fresh white tea and spicy pink pepper, transitioning to a floral heart of rose, jasmine, and iris. The base combines earthy patchouli, vetiver, and sensual musk, creating a sophisticated blend suitable for evening wear or cooler weather occasions. Reviews highlight its velvety texture and balanced composition that projects elegance without being overpowering.

Arlene_witha_Y10/13/25 16:12

I just blind bought this and it arrived today.

I’m not a huge rose scent by itself lover but I’m always looking for rose done differently and i have quite a few that are all very different.

I wanted something vampy and dark for the fall winter season

This delivers!!

It’s a deep dark red rose with the rich earthy sweetness of patchouli that reminds me of coco mademoiselle or coco noir.

But it has this white tea note that reminds me of the spa like aroma of tea fragrances that blends so nicely with the deeper dark rose and earthy sweetness patchouli bringing a tad of softness to all that weight, plus the pink pepper that brightens it up just a tad more and then that lovely woody vetiver in the end. I don’t pick up a lot on the Jasmine but maybe it’s just that well blended bc this isn’t just straight up rose. Yes you smell a lot of the rose but it’s rose done differently due to all those other notes that come together with it so nicely!

This is not a juvenile bubble gum pink candy marshmallow fragrance.

This is adult, this is sophistication, this reminds me of perfume from the 90s.

And I’m all here for it! I love it!!

Gillygal2304/25/25 20:44

As a person who is trying to enjoy the pink roses so popular at the moment, I took a chance on this and am so glad I did. This is a sexy, photorealistic rose scent. The tea and pink pepper keep it sparkling but I really enjoy the grounding of the patchouli and vetiver dry down, with the animalic musk that makes this really gorgeous. This isn’t for the playground - this is sophisticated and grown up. For the price you’d better get the car started - it is very close to the OG and you can go through bottles without guilt.

Morgunvalmua04/18/25 02:31

Tested this side by side with YSL Rouge Velours. One on a wrist, one on the other. I honestly couldn't tell the difference. This is simply almost an identical alternative and its only minor flaw would be a poorer longevity than its original counterpart, but for the price point it's honestly a steal if you're a rose aficionado and I wouldn't mind reapplying more often.

That said, these two rose renditions are simply lovely. The rose in these is red, elegant, mature, feminine and seductive. It's not a young, pink, spring rose for young girls. I'd say it's a very good perfume for a special occasion, a romantic one especially. It's an evening scent. I'd wear this almost year round, perhaps not in summer as it could be quite overpowering. I prefer this in colder weather, that's when it really opens up to its fullest potential. If you love rose fragrances, then this is a must. 🙂

semyonsemyoff04/01/25 00:17

Nose Note #139

The most realistic rose I've smelled in a fragrance so far. We've had some rose dominant fragrances, but this one is just so beautiful. Strange it doesn't have that much attention. The juice color really suits the scent and the bottle altogether looks very classy.

If you are building a small collection, this could be the only one fragrance for colder days. It may not suit underage girls, but it can add a touch of class to anybody.

Personally I wear almost anything, even if it's designed to be feminine. I feel very comfortable wearing this. Women fragrances usually give a man some impression of extra amiability.

For me it happened to be an unexpected gem.

The only downside is that I find the performance to be just average
